# Stage School A1 — targeted refinement completion

Overall architecture/design direction is approved. This one art-refinement pass is complete and awaits the user's final LOD0 visual approval. The candidate remains isolated from the runtime building.

[Open the fourteen-view Unity gallery](review.html).

1. **Roof changes.** Preserved all three masses, pitches, tile-course positions, eaves, low ridge arrangement and terracotta palette. Tile courses are now one editable batched mesh per roof mass. Added small cap-course joints and formed headwall flashing. No architectural redesign.

2. **Variation and repetition.** One deterministic 1024² albedo atlas and one 1024² metallic/smoothness atlas supply 64 restrained firing/mineral samples. Tiles choose atlas cells and mirrored UVs independently. Small hue/value shifts and smoothness variation replace the previous random selection of whole-tile colours. No baked light, grime, damage or new material assets. Variation is deliberately quiet at management distance; the approved geometric course rhythm remains regular.

3. **Construction corrections.** Replaced closed tubular gutters with open half-round troughs, rolled lips, stop ends and outlet sleeves. Moved their centres outward 135 mm to sit beneath the existing drip edge; connected brackets and swan necks follow that adjustment. Downpipe routes, straps and ground discharge positions remain. Added formed metal apron/upstand flashing at the raised central walls. Ridge/hip sections have narrow lap/seam definition rather than uninterrupted long rolls. Main bounds remain unchanged; cap seam relief increases the measured crown by only 0.8 mm. No architectural defect requiring a redesign was identified in this limited review.

4. **Curtains.** Removed cylindrical fold strips, spherical valance lobes and the flat red backing. Added continuous authored velvet panels with gathered waists, varying fold depth, turned edges, weighted hems, sewn scalloped valance, suspension rail, wall-supported brackets and tieback hardware. Maintained burgundy, the same rostrum and 3.1 m neutral backdrop, and approximately the same curtain footprint. The gathered sides reveal more of the wall beside the backdrop at waist height. Roughness is 0.89; no cloth simulation. Entire curtain assembly: 27,440 triangles.

5. **Camera.** New independent camera candidate with cast case, removable covers, paired film magazines, generic three-lens turret, lens rings/glass, side finder, footage dial, hand crank, carry handle, geared head, pan handle and paired timber tripod with connected spreader. It occupies the existing camera position and aims toward the performance area. Camera alone: 14,752 triangles, 12,535 vertices, six semantic renderers. It is an unbranded mechanical interpretation, not a replica or a sound-recording rig.

6. **Studio lights.** Replaced both simplified lamps with instances of one open-face incandescent candidate: hollow drum, rolled stiffeners, rear cooling details, spun reflector, bulb/socket, guard, four sheet shutters, yoke, locking hardware, telescopic standard, connected feet/braces and a short supply lead. Kept the existing light positions and aimed them inward. One lamp: 6,856 triangles, 5,919 vertices, six semantic renderers. The lamps are art props; no new runtime lighting controls were added.

7. **Reusable organization.** `ArtSource/ProductionEquipment1930Candidate/` contains the standalone generator, shared primitive helpers, linked Blender source, generation report and README. `ArtExports/ProductionEquipment1930Candidate/equipment_meshes.json.gz` uses the established semantic packet format. `Assets/SilverScreen/Environment/ProductionEquipment1930Candidate/` contains its independent editor importer, mesh assets and `Prefabs/StudioCamera1930.prefab` / `StudioLamp1930.prefab`. A1 links the collections in Blender and nests their Unity prefabs. Both lamps share all six meshes and materials; only 21,608 unique equipment triangles are stored. Camera Body/Lens/Magazines/Controls/Head/Tripod and lamp Housing/Reflector/Shutters/Yoke/Stand/Cable remain separate. No Stage School dependency inside the equipment generator, no canonical-kit migration, no Stage 1 replacement.

8. **Dressing.** Reception gains a two-level application tray, blotter, inkwell/pen and a modest wire basket behind the desk. Waiting gains two restrained film-publication covers and a leather portfolio. Commons gains portfolio/form review material and a pencil. Audition gains evaluation paperwork/pencil; Director Interview gains a review portfolio and script ribbon. Replaced three generic paperwork fixture instances at reception/waiting/commons instead of layering duplicates. Existing phones, lamps, furniture, headshots, notices and eight waiting seats remain. Public circulation, stage, evaluator positions and interview seating are preserved.
