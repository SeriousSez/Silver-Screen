## Source diagnosis

The old generator combined the CC0 MakeHuman hm08 base with sex/ancestry/body
linear targets, regional facial targets, and MHCLO barycentric garment fitting.
It did not construct the bodies from primitives. C used maximum muscle AND
maximum weight; B used minimum weight and uniform height calibration. This
emphasized generator extremes before establishing an authored art standard.
Subdivision increased smoothness, not design quality. Off-the-shelf clothing
and hair were not individually sculpted/tailored to the supplied 1930 concept.
Early captures also exposed excessive portrait illumination, duplicate LOD
ownership, forward-axis problems, and an unhandled transparent eye surface.
Those technical defects do not explain away the full-art rejection.
