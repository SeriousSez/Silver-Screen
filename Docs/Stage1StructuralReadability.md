# Stage 1 structural cutaway refinement — 2026-09-29

This is a targeted follow-up to Stage1PresentationIntegration.md. The existing

## Completion report

1. **Existing organization.** The five transverse bowstring trusses occur at local Z = -11.2, -5.6, 0, 5.6 and 11.2 m. `Export_PrimaryStructure` also batches their floor-to-roof stanchions, longitudinal purlins and roof-plane bracing. Riveted gussets, cleats, bearing plates and foundation hardware are in `Export_StructuralConnections`. Suspension rods/clamps/turnbuckles share `Export_RiggingPrimary` with the independently spanning rigging grid. Previously all these batches were in RetainedInterior and stayed fully opaque.

2. **Authored groups.** Added five TrussBand groups, five TrussConnections support groups and five TrussHangers nested groups. The authoring recipe separates complete connected components using their local bounds and known source frame stations. It retains whole columns rather than cutting their tops off. Longitudinal members and base connections are excluded from the truss bands. Eighteen additional mesh assets replace three rendered batches, a net increase of fifteen active renderers. There are now 55 visibility groups and 80 derived section meshes in the live prefab.

3. **Generic extension.** VisibilityGroup now has a `Mode` (`Shell` or `StructuralOccluder`), `CutawayOpacity`, `StructuralFocus`, and enter/exit coverage settings. The existing zero-normal retained groups still provide normal visibility. No Stage mesh-name checks were added to runtime logic. Existing shell rules and full-hide behavior remain unchanged.

4. **Selection.** Structural roots use authored local section bounds and a local usable-floor area. Fifty inexpensive finite sightline tests per band determine whether the band crosses views to that area. The floor sampling includes a row beneath each band so thin structures do not disappear between samples when viewed along their span. Orthographic observers use parallel rays. This is an approximation using authored band volumes, not per-triangle occlusion. There is no fixed rule to fade the first two trusses; each band is tested independently against the current observer.

5. **Opacity.** The initial retained material alpha is **0.22**. Tune `CutawayOpacity` on the five structural root groups in the live prefab's BuildingCutawayController; dependent groups inherit it. The reproducible default is authored in Stage1PresentationAuthoring.Configure. Regeneration reapplies that default. Existing material assets and the roof reflection correction are unchanged.

6. **Hysteresis and transition.** A band enters partial cutaway at 4% sampled coverage (at least two of fifty rays). Once selected, it remains selected at 1.5% (one ray) and releases when coverage falls below that. The existing 0.35-second unscaled progression and smooth easing apply to reveal, restoration and camera-side changes. The target opacity is independent of progression, so partial fades do not shorten the configured transition. Cached fade variants remain bound while a band is partially visible.

7. **Fully visible elements.** Non-obstructing trusses, whole support columns, column foundations, longitudinal purlins/bracing, independently spanning rigging, catwalks, guards, stairs, floor, pendant carriers and other retained interior elements remain opaque. No railing-by-railing classification or LOD work was done.

8. **Partially fading elements.** Selected overhead transverse chords and webs, their upper structural gussets/rivets/cleats, and their narrow suspension rods/clamps/turnbuckles can fade. Broad rigging channels and cross-grid battens stay in RetainedInterior. No truss geometry was remodeled, and material/vertex attributes and total triangle counts are preserved.

9. **Attachment inheritance.** TrussBand → TrussConnections → TrussHangers resolves through the generic cached support graph. Every dependent inherits the root's selection and partial opacity, regardless of group order. The same mechanism scales authored dependent lights. Stage wall lamps and roof-shell equipment retain their existing shell ownership. Independently spanning infrastructure is not assigned to one partially faded support.

10. **Interaction versus physics.** Structural groups' authored interaction occluders are filtered when their current opacity is 35% or lower, including while restoring. Their physical colliders stay enabled. This Stage has no separate overhead truss colliders, so no new ones were created; its ground-level column collision remains. A focused generic test confirms nested partial-opacity ray filtering without disabling physics.

11. **Arbitrary yaw.** Observer positions and orthographic directions are transformed into building-local space. Tests cover front/rear selection and restoration at 0°, 37°, 90° and 143°. The runtime sanity used 37°.

12. **Performance.** Five structural roots perform at most 250 simple segment/bounds checks per active frame; dependent groups reuse their root's decision. No runtime mesh analysis, partitioning, mesh creation or per-frame material allocation was introduced. The fifteen additional active renderers and transparent overdraw during partial fades are real costs; this pass did not profile them. All partitioning happens through the existing Editor recipe. No new LOD system.
