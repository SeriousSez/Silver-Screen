# Stage School C1 — applicant arrival and direct hiring

Implemented against production A4/B2, without changing its art, prefab, metadata, material assignments, collision or LODs. No C2/C3/C4 gameplay, commit or push.

## Completion report

1. **Applicant domain/state model.** Extended the existing `Candidate` and `RecruitmentCoordinator`, rather than introducing another person framework. The lifecycle is Arriving → WaitingForRecruitment → Hired, or Departing → Gone. The candidate carries target facility ID, reserved authored slot, waiting/travel elapsed minutes and original profession intent. The coordinator owns arrival countdown and deterministic facility cursor.
2. **Person identity integration.** `PersonProfile` remains the person. `WorkforceRoster.Population` is a small shared domain identity registry for applicants, employees and offsite people. Hiring adds an employment wrapper around the SAME profile and converts the SAME GameObject from CandidateAgent to EmployeeAgent. Departure removes the spawned view after exiting; the person and candidate history survive in domain state.
3. **Arrival cadence and tuning.** Serialized `RecruitmentDriver._talentApplicants` uses `TalentApplicantSettings`: first arrival after **60 simulation minutes** with an available school; subsequent arrivals every **180 minutes**; retry after **30 minutes** when full/unreachable. Maximum active talent applicants **6** across the studio. Waiting expiry **1,440 minutes** (one day); travel/departure recovery timeout **720 minutes**. No available school resets the initial countdown rather than accumulating missed arrivals. These are initial values, not a balancing study.
4. **Capacity policy.** Capacity is bounded both by the global cap and each instance's authored waiting-anchor count. Arriving and waiting people reserve slots; departing people release their slot immediately but continue counting against the active population cap until gone.
5. **Assignment policy.** Deterministic round-robin over registered, available, reachable schools with free slots. No global Stage School singleton and no profession-specific applicant pools.
6. **Exterior reservations.** The six B2 `applicant.exterior.*` transforms are sorted by authored ID. `RecruitmentFacility` owns person-ID-to-slot reservations in domain state; CandidateWaitingAreaView adapts that same reservation map to transforms. Slots are claimed before routing and released on hire, departure, routing failure or facility loss. Carrying retains the slot until the player completes an action.
7. **Arrival/navigation.** Reuses CandidateWorldRouter, CandidateAgent, the StudioBuildLot street-side arrival source, shared NavMesh and human navigation profile. Reachability is checked on arrival attempts, before creating the person. People walk to the school approach and their distinct waiting slot. Reassignment reuses the existing agent. Departures use the lot's world arrival/exit area. Failed or timed-out navigation safely releases active applicant state while preserving identity.
8. **Waiting behavior.** Waiting holds the reserved destination; ordinary employee wandering does not run on applicants. A restrained visual-only lean/turn uses the existing capsule body and local simulation presentation time. It does not move the NavMeshAgent or collider. Pickup restores the body for the existing held-person presentation; hiring removes this applicant visual behavior. Final character animation is not part of C1.
9. **Selection.** Quick left click retains existing person selection. Hover/right-click uses the existing person information cards. Stage School talents do not open the old bulk recruitment panel. That panel and its existing behavior remain available for other applicants.
10. **Carry.** Existing threshold pickup, original release retaining carry, separate-click placement, ground-drop validation and cancellation remain in PersonInteractionController/PersonDragSession. No replacement input controller or bindings. Held status pauses applicant waiting/travel expiry, preserving the reservation while manipulated.
11. **Contextual regions.** A small operational-building adapter reads the four B2 Hiring Hall regions. Generic ContextualDropTarget room geometry and generic building reveal own presentation and placement. ACTOR, DIRECTOR and EXTRA are valid drops for every unhired talent applicant. CREATE / IMPORT TALENT appears greyed with **NOT YET AVAILABLE**, is not eligible for magnetic selection, and cannot execute. Regions are runtime contextual objects outside the LOD branches and never permanent floor art. Employees are ineligible for these C1 actions.
12. **Actor hiring.** A valid contextual drop calls existing RecruitmentCoordinator.Hire with Actor. It validates active applicant/facility, marks hired, releases reservations, updates the person's profession, registers the employment wrapper/converted agent with StudioEmployeeManager and removes active applicant membership.
13. **Director hiring.** The same lifecycle with Director; no evaluator, existing Director, eligibility score or prerequisite employee. The Play Mode proof starts with zero studio employees.
14. **Extra hiring.** The same lifecycle with Extra, using a real person and employee. No filming/crowd system was added.
15. **Professions/proficiencies.** `ProfessionalRole.Unassigned` is appended to the existing enum, preserving previous serialized enum values. Talent generation is neutral: ordinary ability values 0–18, independently per acting/directing/writing, with a 5% chance per ability of 25–40; genre values 0–12. Hiring sets the chosen profession only. The original TalentProfile, genre entries, traits, birth date and appearance/voice identity remain intact. Original neutral intent remains in candidate history. Unrelated proficiencies are not rewritten.
16. **Release/rejection.** The existing generic `RecruitmentCoordinator.Reject(candidate)` works for arriving and waiting people, releases their reservation immediately and routes them offsite. Waiting expiry supplies an automatic departure path. No new rejection-management screen; a dedicated player-facing rejection affordance for talent applicants is deferred. The underlying operation is available to a future small interaction.
17. **School unavailable.** Disable/destruction unregisters the instance and reconciles its applicants immediately. The minute service also checks facility availability. It tries another eligible school and keeps the same person/agent; otherwise it sends the person away. Departing callbacks cannot restore waiting state. No demolition feature was added.
18. **Multiple schools.** Each adapter registers a distinct construction instance ID, its own capacity, reservation dictionary and regions. Applicants record an owner. Hiring at another available Stage School releases the original owner's reservation. Disabling one school does not remove the others.
19. **Simulation clock.** Arrival, expiry and travel recovery use the authoritative OnMinutePassed stream. No Time.timeScale owner was added. Candidate navigation keeps the existing world-speed adapter; idle visuals use LocalPresentationTime. C1 tests verify pause and 1×/2×/3× arrival cadence. Carry/camera controls retain their unscaled input timing.
20. **Save implications.** ApplicantSnapshot now includes profession-neutral intent, target ID, lifecycle status, slot and elapsed waiting/travel minutes. RecruitmentStateSnapshot captures history, arrival countdown and facility cursor. Identity lives in the shared population. These are save-friendly domain records; no disk serializer, restoration pipeline, persistent random-generator restore or new save system was implemented. Future save/load must restore the existing profiles and then rebuild reservations against the same instance/anchor IDs. Carry pose is transient.
21. **UI.** Reused person cards and existing floor-region renderer. Talent cards say no profession has been chosen. The existing information provider already exposes exact skills/genre experience/traits; C1 does not redesign knowledge/confidence. C2 must introduce information discovery/visibility before claiming those values are hidden. Salary expectation remains the existing generic hiring model.
22. **Focused tests.** 17 new C1 cases pass (also 17/17 on the final cleanup rerun), covering operational/reachable gating, no-school backlog, authored/global capacity, distinct pre-arrival reservations, neutral entry-level generation, all three hires, identity/data continuity, release and departure, held expiry, pause/speeds, unavailable-school reassignment/departure, multiple owners, arrival failure and contextual eligibility. Existing recruitment (15), person interaction (8), contextual targets (6), and domain/UI clock checks (4) also pass. Combined focused run: **50/51 passed**. No full project suite was run.
23. **Play Mode sanity.** The dedicated C1 real-input proof **passed** in Unity 6000.6.3f1. An operational A4/B2 fixture and zero studio employees receive clock-driven applicants from the real street source, physically navigate to an authored slot, respond to normal click selection and hold pickup, retain carry on release, show all four contextual regions with only three eligible, survive an LOD switch and hire through a later click. Actor and Director were both exercised. The same profiles AND GameObjects were asserted, reservations released and normal employee walking resumed. Extra is covered by domain tests. Fixture construction used temporary domain setup labor; the arrival and hiring interaction was not bypassed. The first proof stopped at an incorrect assertion counting the disabled affordance as eligible; this was corrected without changing gameplay. Evidence is retained.
24. **Performance.** Facility registration is lifecycle-driven; reachability queries happen at scheduled arrival/reassignment attempts. No per-frame school discovery or anchor regeneration. Waiting updates animate one cached visual transform per person. Existing carry targeting retains its bounded registry/physics behavior. The population/history intentionally retains identity records for departed people; future persistence/world population management will own long-term storage. No whole-game profiling was performed.
25. **Files changed.** See the list below and `ArtReview/StageSchoolC1/changed_files.json`. New scripts have Unity-generated metadata. All 1,574 files in the final A4 art/shared-source preservation baseline retain their hashes, including Studio.unity. No production art, prefab or saved scene was edited for C1; runtime registration follows completed construction.
26. **Known limitations.** The broader existing `SimulationSpeedTests.StudioHudScalesClockPeopleAndAnimationButNotCameraAndPreservesModalPause` failed its real-time motion/animation assertion (normal-speed clock/work ≈1.01; employee ≈0.023, applicant ≈0.390, animation ≈0.134). Its cause was not established in this milestone; it is not reported as passing or proven pre-existing. New C1 clock cases and actual applicant navigation/hiring pass. Prototype capsule characters/idle visuals and fully exposed person proficiencies are inherited presentation constraints. No C2 evaluation, C3 creation/import, C4 reassignment/dismissal, fame or crowd gameplay was added.
27. **Manual checks.** Start a New Studio with tutorial skipped if needed. Build and complete a Stage School; during construction confirm no talent applicants target it. After completion, run one simulation hour: a talent applicant should walk from the street into a waiting pocket. Quick click should select; hover/right click inspects. Hold left click past the normal pickup threshold, release, move over the Hiring Hall: three profession labels and the disabled future label should appear. A later click on ACTOR should hire immediately. Repeat DIRECTOR and EXTRA; inspect employee profession and watch normal movement resume. Carry over the future region: it must not hire/drop there. Test Escape and an ordinary ground drop; an unhired applicant should return to their reserved position. Pause across an arrival deadline, resume at 1×/2×/3×, and confirm modest arrivals without stacking. Let an applicant wait one simulation day and watch departure. With two completed schools, confirm separate ownership; disable one school's root in a development session and verify reassignment/departure and freed reservations. Orbit/zoom while carrying to check camera-relative cutaway and region readability at preferred gameplay distances.
