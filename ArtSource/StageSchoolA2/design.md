# Stage School A2 — architectural expansion

Continue the refined A1 through selective reuse, not uniform scaling. One occupied
floor, finished datum 0.36 m. The A1 entrance, front elevation, steps, lettering,
fanlight, bronze doors and banners remain at their existing coordinates. Approved
camera, lamps and curtain meshes are retained; only placement changes.

Plan envelope: 28 × 24 m, x -14 to +14, front y -9 to rear y +15.
The public frontage remains 22 m wide. Westward projection begins at y +1;
the taller audition wing projects eastward at y +1 and extends to y +15.
The working west wing ends at y +14. Interview ends at y +12, creating a
substantial rear setback between unequal wings. Roof overhangs/service canopy
and entrance steps extend beyond the masonry envelope.

Audition sequence, in metres: evaluator desk y4.0, operator clearance around y6.3,
camera y7.8, two lamps y11.0, performer area y13.4. The existing 5.6 × 2.6 m
rostrum is retained, including its 3.1 m neutral backdrop. Clear lateral access
runs from the southwest room door along the west side of the equipment to the
rostrum. Two people fit within the performance area without enlarging the stage.

Roof hierarchy: unchanged-height entrance crown; lower front wings; raised
central hall with true high glazing; transverse west evaluation/staff hip;
subordinate rear service hip; low common bay roof; intimate interview hip;
taller long audition roof. Connections are stepped headwall/abutment conditions
with formed flashing, not unresolved intersecting equal-height roof planes.
Open gutter runs, outlets and supported downpipes belong to each roof assembly.

All elevations get a stone plinth, restrained coursed lower masonry, recessed
stucco fields, expressed corners and selected pilasters/string courses. Window
surrounds preserve the A1 language but face outward on every elevation. The rear
has a plain covered service entrance, louvers and connected utility details.

No gameplay, second playable floor, runtime promotion, LODs or construction
derivatives. Final architectural approval follows the fourteen-view Unity review.
