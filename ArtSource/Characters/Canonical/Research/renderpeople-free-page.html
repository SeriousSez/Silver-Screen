    Renderpeople Product Range

    Renderpeople offers four different types of product: Posed People, Rigged People, Animated People and 4D People. To get a better idea of all differences, please find the descriptions below.

    3D Posed People

    Experience unmatched scanning precision with our revamped Renderpeople Scanner 2.0, enhanced by cutting‑edge processing technologies and PBR. Our new‑gen Posed People elevate realism to unprecedented levels, bringing your visualizations to life like never before. Flexible materials, streamlined 4K texture maps, and efficient 50k meshes ensure peak performance in every scene.

    3D Posed People Classic

    Posed People are static scanned 3D People. Each of them portrays a specific natural pose that has been scanned in our state-of-the-art 3D scanner. The geometry and high-resolution textures of all Posed 3D People have been manually refined to match the vividness and authenticity of real humans. Posed People include pre-built materials and are ready to render.

    3D Posed People Plus

    Witness unparalleled realism with Posed People Plus. Each scan captures even the most delicate details, delivering a lifelike quality that elevates every project.Our new state-of-the-art photogrammetry scanner and optimised post-processing pipeline ensure that every pixel captured is pushed to its limits. The results are our most detailed and true-to-life 3D people models yet.

    3D Rigged People

    Rigged People are movable and posable scanned 3D People. Each of them includes a skeleton that has already been bound to the geometry (also known as skinning). Our 3D Rigged People can be easily posed or animated in any way you like, making them versatile for use in many fields of application. Motion capturing data can also be applied to quickly create realistic movement.

    3D Animated People

    Our Animated People are moving 3D People. Each of them includes cleaned and optimized motion capture animation that has already been applied and baked onto the skeleton. Animated 3D People are specifically designed to be easy to use, meaning that no animation skills are required for working with them. After importing an Animated People into your 3D scene it starts moving right away.

    4D People

    4D People are volumetrically scanned humans for visualization, VFX, games, VR, AR and everything metaverse. Each model contains a high-res mesh and texture sequence which have been captured in one of the world’s leading volumetric studios operated by Volucap. Our models can be used in all common 3D applications, no plug-ins required.
